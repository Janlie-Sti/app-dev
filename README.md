## About
**Genre:** Action, Drama  
**Network:** ABS-CBN  
**Premiered:** 2015  
**Lead Actor:** Coco Martin  

*A Filipino action-drama about Cardo Dalisay, a police officer from the province who fights corruption and protects his family and the people he cares about.*

## Main Characters
- **Ricardo "Cardo" Dalisay** – the main hero, played by Coco Martin
- **Task Force Agila** – the group Cardo works with
  - Teamwork and loyalty are a big part of the show
- **Cardo's family** – the people he fights to protect
